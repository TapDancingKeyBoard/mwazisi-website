How we work

Four steps, in this order.

Every engagement starts by understanding what you have and ends with a report you can read.

STEP 1

Assess

We review your devices, accounts, backups and risks, and agree priorities with you.

STEP 2

Set up

We fix the basics: monitoring, patching, backup and access, documented as we go.

STEP 3

Support

Your staff get a clear route to help, with issues logged and tracked to closure.

STEP 4

Review

A regular report shows what was done, what is open and what to plan for next.
