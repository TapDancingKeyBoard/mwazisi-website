Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how we work, what we do and how to get started. Can't find your question? Ask us directly.

About us

What does Mwazisi Consultancy do?

We are an IT consultancy and managed IT services provider in Lusaka, Zambia. We look after the computers, email, backups and data of organisations so their teams can get on with their work: day-to-day IT support, Microsoft 365 and email, backup and recovery, networks, devices, data protection, and digital tools.

Who do you work with?

NGOs and international non-profits, development and research programmes, local authorities, law and advisory firms, and small and growing businesses that need dependable IT without a full-time IT department.

Where do you work?

We are based in Lusaka. We visit offices in Lusaka in person and support organisations across Zambia remotely. Work outside Lusaka can be arranged, with travel agreed in advance.

Are you registered?

Yes. Mwazisi Consultancy is registered with PACRA and ZRA, is a licensed Data Processor under Zambia's Data Protection Act, and is a member of the ICT Association of Zambia (ICTAZ).

Who will we deal with?

Every engagement is led by our Principal Consultant, Sam Gondwe. You deal directly with the person doing the work, not a call centre.

Working with us

How does a monthly retainer work?

You pay a fixed monthly fee for an agreed number of support hours. Time is recorded against your account, and every invoice comes with a timesheet showing what was done, for whom and how long it took, plus a monthly report you can read and share.

Do we have to sign up for a retainer?

No. You can also book a quoted project with a fixed scope and price, or ad hoc support when you need it, either on site in Lusaka or remotely.

How do we ask for help?

Email info@mwazisi.com, or call or WhatsApp +260 76 562 8704. Every request is logged and tracked until it is resolved. Retainer clients agree response times as part of their agreement.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your organisation and what you need. Retainers are a fixed monthly fee and projects are quoted in advance, with itemised invoices. Get in touch and we will suggest the right option; where it helps, the first call is free.

We are setting up a new office. Can you help?

Yes. We plan the setup, prepare the budget and quotation, compare internet providers, source laptops, printers and network equipment, set up business email on your own domain, the office network and security, and hand over with staff induction and documentation. We can then look after it every month.

What happens if we stop working with you?

We hand everything over: passwords, admin access and documentation, so your next provider or your own team can take over smoothly.

Data and security

Can you help us comply with the Data Protection Act?

Yes. As a licensed Data Processor, we help organisations understand and meet their duties under the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021: data and risk assessments, policies and records, breach procedures and staff awareness. This supports compliance; it is not legal advice.

Will you keep our information confidential?

Yes. We treat everything we see in your systems as confidential. We only name clients publicly, or show their logos, with their permission, and our case studies leave out client details.

Do you work with Microsoft 365 for NGOs?

Yes. We set up and support Microsoft 365 (email, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint) and help eligible non-profits apply for Microsoft's nonprofit programme.
