How digitally ready is your business?

Answer up to 16 quick questions about how you find customers, handle money, keep records safe and grow. You'll get a scorecard you can download or share, and the next steps that will make the biggest difference.

What the test looks at

  • 📣 Found online: can customers find you and get a reply?
  • 💰 Money and records: bookkeeping, invoices and how customers pay.
  • 🗂️ Ways of working: where files live and how paperwork flows.
  • 🛡️ Safety and backups: what happens if a phone or laptop is lost.
  • 🤝 People and support: who keeps things running, and who else knows how.
